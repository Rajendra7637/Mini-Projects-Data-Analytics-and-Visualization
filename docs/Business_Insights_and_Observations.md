## 1. Project 1: COVID-19 Public Health Analytics

### 1.1 Temporal Wave Dynamics
- **Wave 1 (Ancestral Strain, Q2 2020):** Characterized by high Case Fatality Rates (~2.5% to 3.0%) due to unstandardized clinical treatment protocols, ventilator shortages, and zero population immunity.
- **Wave 2 (Delta Variant, Q2 2021):** Experienced exponential infection spikes with high hospitalizations, reaching peak global monthly fatalities.
- **Wave 3 (Omicron Variant, Q1 2022):** Generated the highest recorded monthly case volume across all continents. However, the Case Fatality Rate fell sharply to <0.5%, demonstrating decoupling of infection incidence from mortality.

### 1.2 Vaccination Impact & Mortality Reduction
- The rollout of vaccines beginning in late 2020 served as the primary clinical turning point.
- Statistical correlation between cumulative vaccination doses and Case Fatality Rate was strongly negative (Pearson $r \approx -0.74$), confirming that mass vaccination drastically truncated fatal outcomes across diverse age demographics.

## 2. Project 2: Commercial Sales & Retail Performance Analytics

### 2.1 Executive Financial Performance
- **Total Revenue:** $11,220,417.57 across 5,000 commercial orders.
- **Total Operating Profit:** $700,155.23 (Net Margin: 6.24%).
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** $2,244.08, indicating high-ticket commercial accounts.

### 2.2 Product Category & Margin Analysis
1. **Technology ($3.9M Sales, $985K Profit, Margin ~25.2%):**
   - **Copiers & Accessories:** Premium profit drivers with margins exceeding 30%. Low discount sensitivity.
   - **Machines:** High gross revenue, but thin margin (~8%) due to higher manufacturing and warranty overhead.
2. **Office Supplies ($3.8M Sales, $1.15M Profit, Margin ~30.3%):**
   - **Paper & Binders:** High-margin cash generators with consistent re-order velocity. Negligible return rates.
3. **Furniture ($3.5M Sales, -$1.43M Profit, Negative Margin):**
   - **Tables & Bookcases:** Persistent negative margin generators ($-8\%$ and $-5\%$). Analysis revealed sales representatives frequently offered discounts between 30% and 50% to win deals, turning these transactions into net financial losses.

### 2.3 Regional Dynamics & Optimization
- **West Region (32% Revenue Share):** Highest sales volume, anchored by California and Washington corporate demand.
- **East Region (28% Revenue Share):** High profitability with dense urban cluster distribution (New York, Massachusetts).
- **Central Region (24% Revenue Share):** Experienced the highest proportion of discounted orders (Texas, Illinois), dragging down net margin.
- **South Region (16% Revenue Share):** Moderate sales volume with opportunity for territorial expansion.
